Course Outline and Syllabus Builder

Fill in the fields, then choose Generate outline. The result is a paste-ready HTML fragment: it starts at the course title and carries no <html> or <body> wrapper, so it drops straight into an existing page.

In any text box: lines starting with - or * become a bulleted list, lines starting with 1. become a numbered list, and other lines become paragraphs.

To add a link, put the wording in square brackets and the address in round brackets straight after it, with no space between:

[BC Transfer Guide](https://www.bctransferguide.ca)

Document type

A syllabus carries everything an outline does, plus the instructor, the term, the weekly schedule, and dated assessments.

Course identification

A single sentence that sits directly under the course title. Keep it shorter and plainer than the calendar description below.

Enter these as hours per week. A field left blank or set to 0 is left out of the document, and a total appears only once two kinds of hour are entered.

Continuing Studies courses may instead enter each field as a total for the whole course rather than per week. Whichever basis you use, use the same one for all three fields.

Course materials and textbooks

Write your texts on the lines below the sentence already in the box, one per line, each starting with -. List every required material here, even the free ones. Export format

A syllabus can go out as plain HTML or as a Brightspace (D2L) page. An outline always goes to the College website as plain HTML, so this choice does not apply to one and is not shown.

Course Outline and Syllabus Builder

Fill in the fields, then generate the HTML. Blank fields are left out of the export. Sections required by Policy F1003 — Course Outline and Syllabus that need no input from you, such as the College policy list, are added automatically.

In any text box: lines starting with - or * become a bulleted list, lines starting with 1. become a numbered list, and other lines become paragraphs.

To add a link, put the wording in square brackets and the address in round brackets straight after it, with no space between:

[BC Transfer Guide](https://www.bctransferguide.ca)
